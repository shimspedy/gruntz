## Description
Train like a soldier. Gruntz is the military fitness app that turns test prep into one clear daily mission — with a program, a workout log, and a readiness score that shows exactly where you stand.

PASS YOUR FITNESS TEST
• Track the Army Fitness Test (AFT), Marine Corps PFT and CFT, Navy PRT, Air Force PT and more
• Log event scores and see your readiness percentage and days until test day
• Find your weakest event and train it first

FOLLOW A REAL PROGRAM
• Structured 8-week programs built for readiness, selection and hard events
• Today's mission is ready when you open the app — no guesswork
• Keep your streak alive and climb from Recruit to Apex

LOG EVERY SET
• Fast set logging with weight, reps and time
• Built-in rest timer with quick adjustments
• Add or swap exercises mid-workout

412 EXERCISE VIDEOS
• A custom video for every movement, with muscles worked, step-by-step form and common mistakes
• Search by name, muscle or equipment

PLAN YOUR OWN WORKOUTS
• Build routines from the full library and schedule them on your training days
• See muscle distribution for every workout

RUN & RUCK
• GPS distance, pace and elevation for runs and loaded rucks
• Audio cues that duck your music instead of stopping it

EARN YOUR RANK
• XP, levels, achievements and a muscle map of everything you've trained

GRUNTZ PRO
